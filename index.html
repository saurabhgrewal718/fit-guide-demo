What the shopper gets

Size advice

Their size, with the reason

Height and chest (or hips) in cm or inches give the best size from the store's chart, with how much room each size leaves and why one is best.

Camera fit guide

See the sizes on yourself

Outlines of the best size and the one they picked, drawn to scale on the live camera picture or a photo, with the difference in cm.

Measure me

No tape measure needed

A two-step scan (facing the camera, then sideways) estimates chest, waist and hips from the body's outline. Most accurate standing 2–3 m back.

  • Camera: allow access when asked. On a laptop, stand back so you're in view down to your hips.
  • Phones: works in Safari and Chrome. Prop the phone up and step back.
  • Privacy: the camera picture never leaves your device, and measurements are remembered only in your browser.
  • First use: the body-tracking models (about 10 MB) download once, then load from the browser's cache.
